You're invited to Dayleaf

A friend keeps their journal in Dayleaf — a diary that lives on a calendar. Use their code in the app and you both get a month of Dayleaf Pro.

······

  1. Install Dayleaf and sign in with Apple or Google.
  2. Open Settings → Invite a friend → “Have a friend's code?” — the code is pasted for you.
  3. Write for three days. Your friend gets their month too.

Codes work during the first week of a new account. Up to 12 rewarded invites per person per year.
