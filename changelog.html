2.1.0 — The wrist, the desk, and the world

August 29, 2026
  • Apple Watch app (Dayleaf Pro): dictate entries, record voice memos, see daily, weekly & monthly goals, and set a one-tap quick entry — right from your wrist
  • The World Is Leafing — a living globe of anonymous sparks, one for every person writing right now
  • Writing about a past day? Photos taken that day now appear right in the editor — attach them with one tap
  • Attach audio from Files — bring in your Voice Memos
  • Scrolling calendar: tap an empty day to start writing instantly
  • Polish: day view layout fixes and smoother theme switching
  • On This Day: revisit what you wrote on this date across the years
  • Dayleaf Pro: yours forever with a single purchase — or from $0.99/month. Extra themes, custom stickers, watch app and more; everything you already use stays free

2.0.0 — Dayleaf 2.0

August 12, 2026

Dayleaf 2.0 — a new name, and your journal comes home.

  • Import from Day One — bring your entire journal over in minutes — photos, videos and audio included (Settings → Import)
  • Add photos and videos to your entries — they appear right on your calendar
  • Record voice notes on the spot and play them back from any entry
  • 7 hand-crafted themes — Paper, Forest, Ocean, Lavender, Rosé, Ink & Gold, Sunset — each with its own light and dark face
  • Day stickers — Birthday, Pay day, Trip and more, right on your calendar days; design your own too
  • Make each planner yours: icon, font, text size, and any color you like (Settings → Manage Planners)
  • Daily reminder — pick a time and never miss a day (Settings → Reminders)
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Back up and restore with iCloud — your entries stay in your own Apple account, private to you
  • Feedback board in Settings — post ideas and the developer replies
  • A beautiful new welcome tour and a cleaner icon
  • Move entries between planners
  • New: photo calendar view and continuous month scrolling

1.4.0 — Rebuilt from the ground up

February 13, 2026

A big update — rebuilt from the ground up as a fully native app, faster and smoother everywhere.

NEW

  • Goals: set daily, weekly, and monthly goals right on your calendar, check them off as you go, and fold them away when you want a clean view.
  • Notes & Reflection: a space on every day, week, and month to look back and write how it really went.
  • Visible zoom controls plus pinch-to-zoom on the month grid.
  • Reorder the entries of any day, exactly the way you want them.
  • Long-press any date to start a new entry instantly.

IMPROVED

  • Bigger, more readable calendar text.
  • Tap any entry to edit; edit and delete buttons right on each entry.
  • Cloud backup now includes your goals, notes, and entry order.
  • Countless fixes and polish throughout.
