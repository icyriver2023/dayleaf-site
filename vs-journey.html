Dayleaf vs Journey

Facts checked October 26, 2026 · Written by the team behind Dayleaf, so read accordingly; we've tried to be fair, and we say where Journey is the better choice.

Journey has been around since 2014 and runs almost everywhere: phones, desktops and the web. Dayleaf is narrower in platforms and deeper in one idea, a journal laid out like a paper planner, sold for one price.

Where Journey is genuinely better

  • Every platform, including the web. If you write from a work PC or a browser, Journey is the safer pick.
  • Coach programs: 60+ guided multi-day journaling programs (membership).
  • Mood tracking and a map of entries built in.
  • Capture from WhatsApp or email, and an optional AI companion.

Where Dayleaf is genuinely better

  • One simple price. $19.99 once for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, against $49.99 a year or $199 lifetime.
  • The calendar is the journal. Entries, photos, stickers and goals live on month and week pages.
  • Apple Watch app that starts an entry with no taps, and voice notes that become searchable text.
  • Local first. Your journal lives on your device and backs up to your own iCloud or Google Drive.

Choose Journey if…

you need Windows, Linux or a web app, or want guided coaching programs.

Choose Dayleaf if…

you use Apple devices or Android, think in calendars rather than feeds, and would rather pay once than keep a membership.

FAQ

How much does Journey cost compared with Dayleaf?

Journey membership is $49.99 a year (or $6.99 a month, $199 lifetime). Dayleaf Pro is $19.99 once for every Apple device, or $9.99 a year.

Does Dayleaf have a web app like Journey?

No. Dayleaf runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android.

Can I switch from Journey to Dayleaf?

Dayleaf imports Day One exports today; a Journey importer is planned.
