What's new in Dayleaf 2.2

Release notes · September 11, 2026

Dayleaf 2.2 is about the seam between your day and your journal: the most common actions now take one tap, and a few take none.

📱 Home Screen & Lock Screen widgets

The Today widget shows the date, your streak, today's goals and stickers — and goals tick off right on the widget. Sticker Stamp widgets stamp one sticker on today with a single tap; add a row of them for whatever you count. Here's every widget and what it does.

⌚️ Sticker stamps from the watch face

Add the Sticker Stamp complication, choose its sticker right on the face the way World Clock picks a city, and every tap stamps today. Repeat taps count up: water ×5. Your most-used stickers fill the slots automatically, a second complication carries the rest, and a Stickers complication opens the full grid. On Series 9 and later, Double Tap stamps without touching the screen at all — the basis of the distraction counter.

🎨 Seven new themes, fourteen in all

Olive, Retro, Clay, Frost, Linen, Kraft and Pop — each with a light and a dark face, and each with its own construction. Olive floats outlined wall-calendar cells, Retro leads with a poster-sized month and print rules, Clay embosses soft tiles, Linen keeps only ruled lines, and Kraft frames photos as perforated stamps.

🏷 Stickers, everywhere

Up to six per day on the calendar, one beside the date and the rest wrapping below. The timeline shows each day's stickers; tap a chip to filter, or search by sticker name. Days with stickers but no entry still appear, with an invitation to write.

🌍 A brighter world

The World Is Leafing globe has richer colour and sparks you can actually see, and you can zoom right down to the city.

🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt

Dayleaf now speaks Vietnamese — the whole app, the watch, and the App Store listing. Eleven languages in all.

What's next

Real-time iCloud sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac is the headline of 2.3. Until then, iCloud backup and restore carries your journal between devices.
