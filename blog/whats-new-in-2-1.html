What's new in Dayleaf 2.1

Release notes · August 29, 2026

Dayleaf 2.1 is our biggest release yet — the journal now lives everywhere you do.

⌚️ Apple Watch

Dictate entries, record voice memos with automatic transcripts, check off goals, and set a one-tap capture that starts the moment you open the app.

💻 Mac

The full journal at home on your desktop — same planners, themes, and calendar.

📸 Photos from that day

Writing about last Saturday? The photos you took that day appear right in the editor. One tap attaches them — no digging through your library.

🌍 The World Is Leafing

A slowly turning globe where every green spark is a real person writing right now. Completely anonymous — a city-level glow and nothing more. Writing is solitary; it doesn't have to feel lonely.

🕰 On This Day

Revisit what you wrote on this date across the years, right where you write today.

🍃 Dayleaf Pro

All seven themes, custom stickers, per-planner fonts, ten photos per entry, voice notes, the watch app, and unlimited planners. Yours forever for $14.99 — or from $0.99/month if you prefer. Everything you already use stays free: your journal, import, and backups are never locked.
